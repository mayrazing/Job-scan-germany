About IDnow

IDnow builds digital identity and fraud-prevention products for European businesses. Its teams operate across Germany, the United Kingdom, Romania, and France.

Role

The engineer works on secure, high-volume backend services for an identity-verification platform. The role includes architecture input, technical guidance, and collaboration across product, UX, DevOps, and QA.

Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain Java applications and microservices.
  • Contribute to scalable system design and architecture decisions.
  • Investigate performance problems in high-volume transaction flows.
  • Review code and support less-experienced engineers.

Preferred experience

  • At least five years of professional software development focused on Java.
  • Java EE plus Spring, Spring Boot, or Play Framework.
  • JVM performance work, REST APIs, and relational databases such as MySQL or PostgreSQL.
  • JUnit, Mockito, TDD, CI/CD, Git, Maven, Docker, and Agile delivery.

Bonus skills

  • Kubernetes, AWS, or microservice architecture.
  • Security-sensitive products, including identity or FinTech systems.
  • Kafka, RabbitMQ, or other event-driven technology.
